REAL PROPERTY / SOURCE LISTINGS

Land for Sale Gateway.

Explore real land listings and property photographs from established marketplaces. Listings open at their source; prices, availability, images and rights must be verified with the seller.

NORTHSTAR does not currently have a licensed live listing feed or rights to display listing photos inside its own cards. A licensed IDX/MLS or land-data integration is needed for that next step. Never rely on photos alone to assess water, access, flood risk, zoning or parcel boundaries.

NATIONAL SITUATIONAL AWARENESS

U.S. Hazard Intelligence.

Live public National Weather Service alerts and USGS earthquakes, including a geographic map and state filter. Conditions change quickly; official emergency alerts take priority.

Coverage is incomplete: this feed does not assess crime, civil unrest, infrastructure failures, evacuation orders or every wildfire. No area is labeled simply safe or unsafe. Check official emergency alert guidance, local emergency management and evacuation notices before making decisions.
